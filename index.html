The Album

Back on Track
A concept album about breaks,
detours and new beginnings
Cover placeholder

Ten original songs, one common thread: "Back on Track" tells of setbacks, detours and the moment you find your way back to the stage – and back to life. Recorded in a Luxembourg studio, produced with the guidance of US producer Ross Petersen. Many songs are enriched by guest musicians on accordion, bagpipes, harmonica and more.

Rooted in the sound of Tom Petty, Bruce Springsteen, John Mellencamp and Bob Seger – Fender Telecaster & harmonica included.

About Me

Georges Liesch is a guitarist, singer and songwriter from Luxembourg. After early years of playing originals in the 80s (Peavey Hawk) and a stretch as a cover-band guitarist (Chorus Line), he returned to music in 2015 – graduating in electric guitar at the Differdange music school, with ongoing vocal and bagpipe studies.

Since 2019 he is the singer and guitarist of FAYE and the MOCKINGBIRDS (repertoire from the 1960s to today). "Back on Track" is his first solo album.
